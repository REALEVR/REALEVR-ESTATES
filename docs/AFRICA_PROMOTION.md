# Promoting RealEVR Estates across Africa (and abroad)

Two audiences, two messages:

| Who | Where they are | What we say | What the site shows them |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Renters, buyers, BnB guests | Anywhere in the 54 African countries | Walk through real homes near you first, then message the owner on WhatsApp | The normal home page, ordered by their city and country |
| Anyone with property to rent, sell or auction | **Outside Africa** (diaspora, investors, managers) | **List your property in Africa free, with a 360° tour** | A welcome built only around free listing: rent, BnB, sale, and bank sales through our team |

The site tells the two apart from the browser's time zone (no prompt, nothing stored beyond the
existing place cookie). A visitor from outside Africa who wants to browse can press "Browse listings" and gets
the normal site. Properties must be in Africa; we do not list property elsewhere.

**What this document is not:** no ads are bought and nobody is messaged in bulk. Real people, in
groups where they are members, with the admin's permission. Scraped-number blasts get the WhatsApp
number banned.

## Where to post, by audience

- **Africa, renters and buyers:** estate and building WhatsApp groups, university housing groups,
  neighbourhood Facebook groups, city subreddits, local X communities, a 20-second tour clip on TikTok/Reels/Shorts.
- **Africa, landlords and agents:** direct one-to-one messages to agents and caretakers from public listings;
  property-owner associations; SACCOs; chambers of commerce.
- **Outside Africa:** diaspora associations by country and city (Ugandans in the UK, Nigerians in Houston,
  Kenyans in Canada...), church and mosque community groups, alumni networks, remittance and
  "building back home" Facebook groups, LinkedIn groups for diaspora professionals and investors. Lead with
  the free listing and the 360° tour (a relative can show it to the buyer); do not lead with browsing.

Add `?utm_source=<group name>` to every link so Admin > Leads shows which channel worked.

## Priorities

1. Pick **three countries** for the first six weeks (suggest Uganda, Kenya and Nigeria, where the platform and its language already fit), and ten cities between them. Thin coverage across 54 countries reads as empty everywhere.
2. For each: 20 listings first (landlords and agents), then renters.
3. Run the diaspora push in parallel: it costs nothing and each listing it brings makes the renter pages better.
4. Look at Admin > Leads every Friday: "What to add" shows which country and city people asked for and did not find.
5. Add the next country only when the first three have listings with tours and weekly Kevin conversations.
